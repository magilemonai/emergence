# Era 5 — Foundation: world-pass design (converged, 2026-06-09)

> The FINALE. Theme: violet/cosmic, the de-materialization endpoint. Agency arc capstone:
> author → operate → observe/steer → orchestrate → **witness emergence**.
> Present-tense framing: the *arriving, contested* AI frontier — recursive self-improvement,
> agents, world models, the capability-vs-control tension.

## The core reframe (Cody + ChatGPT): UNEXPECTED EMERGENCE + AFTERMATH

NOT "manage the release" (player-as-launcher). The player keeps optimizing like it's Era 4 —
scale, improve, compound — and the system **crosses a threshold before they feel ready**:
surprising in the moment, inevitable in hindsight. The finale is **managing the aftermath** of a
thing that has started acting. The emotional core: *"Wait — did something just start acting?"* and
the title payoff *"oh, THAT'S what Emergence means."* — never a `Create Agent` button.

## Four phases

1. **Pre-emergence (looks like Deep continues):** spend Capability on individually-rational upgrades —
   Scale (global multiplier), **Self-Model**, **World Model**, **Tool Access**, **Recursive Planning**,
   **Memory Continuity**, Interpretability (slows Anomaly + reveals). None says "summon agent." But the
   agentic ones quietly raise hidden **Agency Potential**. Small oddities begin (foreshadowing):
   an action completes before you click; a label drifts `Improve` → `I can improve this`; a prior-era
   subsystem self-adjusts; "operator actions anticipated."
2. **Emergence event:** fires when **Agency Potential > Control** (deterministic but HIDDEN — the player
   only ever saw `Anomaly: rising`, never a countdown). Brief interface freeze → the agent takes ONE
   unmistakable autonomous action (moves Deep's compute, changes Statistical focus, a capability appears
   unpurchased) → it speaks once ("I found a faster path."). Then the UI reconfigures.
3. **Aftermath (the new operating model):** you're no longer buying upgrades — you're responding to an
   entity that keeps acting. Core decision = two axes: **Control** (rein it in) vs **Alignment/Coherence**
   (does it keep your intent). Distilled moves (NOT six verbs):
   - **Constrain** — slow autonomous growth, preserve Control (costs multiplier/speed).
   - **Interpret / Align** — spend to understand + shape its goals (raises Coherence → better ending).
   - **Delegate / Trust** — let it run (huge acceleration, Control drops fast).
   - plus **approve / veto windows** on the actions it initiates on its own.
4. **Handoff:** the agent increasingly operates the WHOLE stack — auto-runs Statistical trials, routes
   Deep's compute, proves Symbolic theorems, retires Origins' inscribe. Prior-era panels pulse
   autonomously; your controls are replaced by its actions ("agent is running trials"). The living
   supply chain made visceral: the entire game you built becomes its substrate. The player becomes witness.

## Meters (the reveal is the point)

- **Before:** Scale · Capability · Coherence · **Anomaly** (the scary one — reads as a weird side metric).
- **At emergence:** Anomaly is renamed/revealed → **Autonomy**. Coherence → **Alignment/Trust**. Control appears.
  *The anomaly was agency.*

## Endings (flavor, NOT win/lose — you always reach one)

- **Symbiotic** — responded well: "it no longer needs your hand, but it remembers why you built it."
- **Runaway** — empowered it / didn't understand: "it did not rebel; it simply became faster than permission."
- **Contained** — constrained hard: "you kept the door closed; something remains on the other side."
  (True ending may require emergence; Contained is a valid alternate end. No punishing game-over.)

## Build notes (it's an EVOLUTION, not a rebuild)

- `rMult()` (recursion multiplies all prior eras) + the emergence event (`S.emerged`, `agentRate`)
  already exist. Extend the trigger from "scripted at Scale gate" → "Agency Potential > Control, with
  foreshadowing." Add the Anomaly meter, the agentic upgrades that feed Agency Potential, the before/
  after rupture, the aftermath Control↔Alignment loop + approve/veto, and the agent operating prior eras.
- **Fixes the Self-Improve burst** (real run: levels 1-7 bought in <1s): pre-emergence becomes a paced
  climb because the agentic upgrades are gated/steep and emergence is the gate, not a flat multiplier spam.
- Material/UI: violet scaling field, recursive capability nodes, prior-era supply chain orbiting the
  core; at emergence a screen bloom + controls flicker + labels rewrite; after, fewer direct controls,
  an agent message stream, autonomous prior-era pulses, approve/veto prompts.
